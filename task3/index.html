Steve Jobs

About Steve Jobs

Steve Jobs was born on February 24, 1955 in San Francisco, USA, and died on October 5, 2011 at the age of 56. He was an entrepreneur and one of the founders of Apple.

Key life events

  1. 1976 - founded Apple together with Steve Wozniak and Ronald Wayne.
  2. 1984 — Apple introduced the Macintosh computer.
  3. 1997 — Jobs returned to Apple after leaving the company in 1985.
  4. 2007 — Introduced the first iPhone.

Achievements

  • Helped make Apple one of the world's most influential technology companies.
  • Led the development and promotion of the Macintosh.
  • Introduced the iPod, which had a major impact on the digital music industry.
  • Launched the iPhone, which greatly influenced the smartphone industry.
